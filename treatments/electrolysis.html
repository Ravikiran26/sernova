Electrolysis Hair Removal

Permanent hair removal.Any hair colour.

Electrolysis at SRENOVA treats each hair follicle individually with a fine sterile probe and a controlled current, so it works on hair that laser cannot target well.

It suits fine, light, blonde, red, grey or white hair, small hormonal areas such as the chin and upper lip, and shaping of brows.

What It Helps With

For hair that laser does not treat well.

Laser targets dark pigment, so it works poorly on light, grey or very fine hair. Electrolysis works on the follicle itself, so hair colour and skin tone matter much less.

Because every follicle is treated by hand, it is best for small or medium areas, or for finishing off stubborn hairs after a course of laser.

Permanent, but gradual.

Each treated follicle stops growing, but hair grows in cycles, so a course of sessions is needed to treat every hair in the area.

Before You Book

Questions worth asking.

Clear answers help clients compare options with more confidence.

Is electrolysis really permanent?

Yes. Electrolysis is a recognised method of permanent hair removal. Once a follicle is fully treated it does not grow hair again, but every follicle in the area must be treated, which takes a course of sessions.

Does it hurt?

Most people feel a brief warm or stinging sensation at each follicle. Sensitivity varies by area, and the clinic can discuss ways to keep you comfortable.

Can it treat grey or white hair?

Yes. This is one of the main reasons to choose electrolysis, because laser needs dark pigment in the hair to work.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the size of the area, how many hairs there are and any hormonal cause of growth. The clinic gives a realistic estimate after assessment.

Should I shave before my session?

No. The hair needs to be visible so each follicle can be found, so avoid shaving, waxing or threading the area for a few days beforehand. The clinic will confirm what to do.
