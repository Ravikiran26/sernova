Body Contouring

Shape refinement with realistic planning.Contours, not weight loss.

Body contouring at SRENOVA focuses on proportion, stubborn fat pockets, loose skin and contour imbalance rather than generic weight-loss promises.

The plan may involve non-surgical devices, liposuction, tightening or surgical contouring depending on anatomy, skin quality and goals.

What It Helps With

For stubborn areas that do not change easily.

Contour concerns can come from localized fat, loose skin, poor elasticity, post-pregnancy change or weight fluctuation. Each needs a different solution.

Clients are guided on whether a subtle non-surgical route is realistic or whether surgical planning would be more honest.

01Stubborn abdomen, flank or thigh fat
02Post-weight-loss looseness

Before You Book

Questions worth asking.

Clear answers help clients compare options with more confidence.

Is body contouring weight loss?

No. It is for shape refinement and selected stubborn areas, not general weight reduction.

Can loose skin be fixed without surgery?

Mild laxity may improve with some methods, but significant loose skin often needs surgical discussion.

Which area can be treated?

It depends on anatomy, skin quality, health history and goals. Common areas include abdomen, flanks, thighs, arms and back.

Will fat come back?

Weight gain can affect treated and untreated areas. Maintenance habits matter after contouring.
