Skin Rejuvenation

Brighter skin, naturally refreshed.Planned by assessment.

Skin rejuvenation at SRENOVA is designed for people who want healthier-looking, brighter and more even skin while keeping the result natural. Your plan may include clinical facials, peels, microneedling, skin boosters or laser-based options depending on your concern.

Start with what you notice in the mirror - dullness, texture, pigmentation, fine lines, dryness or tired-looking skin - and let the clinician guide the right route.

What It Helps With

For skin that looks dull, uneven or tired.

Skin can lose freshness because of sun exposure, pigmentation, dehydration, early ageing, acne marks, texture irregularity or a routine that is not matching your skin. A rejuvenation plan brings the focus back to skin quality instead of simply adding more products or choosing a procedure blindly.

The goal is visible refinement: brighter-looking skin, smoother texture, more even tone and a healthier overall appearance. The treatment intensity is adjusted to your comfort, skin type, schedule and recovery tolerance.

Rejuvenation Pathways

For glow, tone, hydration and refined skin quality.

This page should feel different from acne care. Rejuvenation is planned around freshness, radiance and maintenance rather than active breakout control.

Instant freshness

Hydra-glow care

Gentle cleansing, hydration and barrier-supportive care for dull or tired-looking skin, especially before events or seasonal refreshes.

Deep hydration

Skin boosters

May be discussed for crepey dryness, reduced bounce and skin that needs hydration support beyond routine facials.

Brightness

Brightening peels

Selected superficial peels may support glow, mild uneven tone and dullness when the skin barrier is stable.

Refinement

Laser toning

Energy-based rejuvenation may be considered for selected tone, texture or collagen-support goals after skin-type screening.

Responsible planning: Not every visitor needs an intensive procedure. For rejuvenation, the better plan may be a lighter glow treatment, routine correction, sunscreen, or a staged maintenance program.

Before You Book

Questions worth asking.

Clear answers help clients feel confident, especially when comparing treatments online.

Which skin rejuvenation treatment is best for me?

That depends on your skin concern, skin type, sensitivity, medical history and available downtime. You do not need to choose the procedure before consultation.

Can skin rejuvenation help with dullness and pigmentation?

It may help selected dullness, tanning and mild uneven tone, but persistent pigmentation needs diagnosis and strict sun protection. The clinician will explain whether rejuvenation or a pigmentation-focused plan is better.

Will my skin peel or look red afterwards?

Recovery depends on the treatment used. A gentle facial may have little visible downtime, while peels, microneedling or laser procedures can involve redness, peeling or temporary sensitivity.

How soon can I do it before an event?

Timing depends on the procedure. Gentle glow treatments may be planned closer to an event, while peels, microneedling and lasers usually need more recovery time and should be planned earlier.

What should I avoid before consultation?

Tell the clinic about active acne, infections, recent procedures, allergies, medications, pregnancy or breastfeeding, and strong skincare actives such as retinoids or exfoliating acids.
