Before You Book

Questions worth asking.

Clear answers help clients compare options with more confidence.

Is laser hair removal permanent?

It is best described as long-term hair reduction. Some maintenance may be needed over time.

Can I wax before laser?

Usually no. Waxing removes the hair root that laser targets. Shaving guidance should be followed instead.

Is it safe for Indian skin?

It can be, when the device and settings are selected appropriately and recent tanning or irritation is considered.

How many sessions do I need?

It varies by area, hair thickness, hormones and response. The clinic should explain a realistic course after assessment.
