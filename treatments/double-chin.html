Double Chin

Submental fat reduction for a defined jawline.Non-surgical and surgical options assessed individually.

A double chin, or submental fat, can be addressed through non-surgical treatments, minimally invasive injections or surgical liposuction depending on the amount of fat and skin laxity present.

SRENOVA assesses the degree of fat, skin quality and facial structure to recommend the most appropriate approach.

What It Addresses

For people wanting to reduce chin fat and define the jawline.

Submental fat may be genetic, related to weight or associated with mild skin laxity — and often does not respond to diet or exercise.

The correct approach depends on whether the issue is mainly fat, skin or a combination of both.

Before You Book

Questions worth asking.

Clear answers help compare options with confidence.

Will exercise reduce a double chin?

For many people, submental fat is resistant to diet and exercise. Assessment determines whether a treatment approach is appropriate.

How many injectable sessions are needed?

Fat-dissolving injections typically require two to four sessions spaced several weeks apart. Response is assessed progressively.

Is the result permanent?

Fat cells removed or destroyed do not return, but weight gain can create new fat deposits over time.

Which option gives the fastest result?

Surgical liposuction gives the most immediate and significant result. Non-surgical options produce gradual improvement over several months.
