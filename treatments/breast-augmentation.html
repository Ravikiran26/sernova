Before You Book

Questions worth asking.

Clear answers help compare options with confidence.

What is the difference between implants and fat transfer?

Implants offer predictable, larger volume change. Fat transfer is subtler, uses your own tissue, and is limited by available donor fat.

Am I a candidate for fat transfer?

Enough donor fat in suitable areas and modest volume goals are needed. This is assessed at consultation.

What happens to fat transfer results over time?

Some absorption is expected. Results stabilise over several months and long-term outcome is discussed at consultation.

Can I combine both methods?

In some cases fat can be used alongside implants to soften edges or improve symmetry. This is planned individually.
