Mummy Makeover

Post-pregnancy body restoration, planned carefully.Combined procedures tailored to your concerns.

A mummy makeover combines two or more procedures — typically addressing the abdomen, breasts and sometimes other areas — to restore pre-pregnancy body contour in a single surgical journey.

SRENOVA plans each makeover individually, considering which procedures are appropriate, the order of surgery, safety and realistic recovery expectations.

What It Addresses

For women wanting to restore body confidence after pregnancy.

Pregnancy and breastfeeding can cause abdominal wall changes, breast volume loss, skin laxity and localised fat deposits that resist diet and exercise.

A makeover plan addresses each concern with the most appropriate procedure, not a fixed package.

01Loose abdominal skin and muscle separation
02Breast volume loss or sagging after breastfeeding
03Persistent localised fat deposits

Before You Book

Questions worth asking.

Clear answers help compare options with confidence.

When is the right time for a mummy makeover?

When you have finished having children, are at a stable weight and are in good health. Pregnancy after a makeover can affect results.

Can all procedures be done at once?

In many cases yes, but surgical duration and safety limits mean some combinations may be staged across two operations.

How long is the recovery?

Recovery varies by procedures included. Planning for at least 4–6 weeks of limited activity is typical for a combined case.

Will I have scars?

Yes. Scar placement and management are discussed in advance. Most scars are positioned to be concealed by clothing.
