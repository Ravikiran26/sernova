Before You Book

Questions worth asking.

Clear answers help clients compare options with more confidence.

Will I look frozen or overfilled?

The aim is conservative, anatomy-led improvement. Dose, placement and treatment choice should preserve expression and proportion.

Do I need Botox or fillers?

Not necessarily. Some concerns are better treated with skincare, boosters, peels or devices. Consultation separates the cause before choosing.

When should anti-aging treatment start?

There is no fixed age. Suitability depends on concern, skin quality, anatomy, medical history and expectations.

Can treatments be combined?

Yes, but combinations should be staged and explained clearly, especially when injectables and device-based treatments are involved.
