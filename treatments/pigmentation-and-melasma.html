Pigmentation & Melasma

Clearer tone, carefully planned.Melasma needs patience.

Pigmentation treatment at SRENOVA starts by identifying the pattern: melasma, sun spots, tanning, post-inflammatory pigmentation or uneven tone after acne.

Because Indian skin can pigment easily, the plan is deliberately gradual: medical skincare, sun protection, selected peels and laser toning only when suitable.

Before You Book

Questions worth asking.

Clear answers help clients compare options with more confidence.

Can melasma be removed permanently?

Melasma often recurs, especially with sun, heat or hormonal triggers. Treatment aims to improve and maintain tone rather than promise permanent removal.

Is laser safe for pigmentation?

Laser may help selected cases, but it is not suitable for everyone. Skin type, pigment pattern and previous reactions must be assessed first.

Why is sunscreen so important?

Sun and visible light can darken pigmentation again. Daily photoprotection is part of treatment, not an optional extra.

Can I use peels if my skin is sensitive?

Possibly, but the skin barrier may need preparation first. The clinician should choose peel type and strength according to tolerance.
