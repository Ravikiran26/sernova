Before You Book

Questions worth asking.

Clear answers help clients compare options with more confidence.

Will Botox remove all wrinkles?

Botox is mainly for selected movement lines. Static lines, skin texture and volume changes may need other approaches.

Are fillers permanent?

Most commonly used dermal fillers are temporary. The clinician should discuss expected longevity and maintenance.

Will it hurt?

Discomfort is usually brief, but sensitivity differs by area and person.

What should I avoid after injectables?

Aftercare varies, but the clinic should explain activity, pressure, heat and skincare precautions before you leave.
