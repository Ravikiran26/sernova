Before You Book

Questions worth asking.

Clear answers help compare options with confidence.

Do I need surgery to lose weight?

Not necessarily. Non-surgical and medication-assisted options work well for many people. The right choice depends on your health profile.

What are weight-loss injectables?

GLP-1 receptor agonists and similar medications can assist weight loss when used under medical supervision.

Who qualifies for bariatric surgery?

Eligibility typically depends on BMI, related health conditions and failure of non-surgical methods. A full medical assessment is required.

What happens after significant weight loss?

Body contouring procedures can address loose skin and reshape the body once weight is stable for at least six months.
