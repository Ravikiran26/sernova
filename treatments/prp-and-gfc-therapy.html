PRP / GFC Therapy

Regenerative support for selected cases.Diagnosis still comes first.

PRP and GFC use processed blood-derived components to support repair signalling in selected scalp and skin concerns.

At SRENOVA, they are positioned as supportive therapies, not miracle shortcuts. Suitability depends on diagnosis, stage, expectations and combination planning.

What It Helps With

For early thinning, density support and selected skin quality goals.

Before You Book

Questions worth asking.

Clear answers help clients compare options with more confidence.

What is the difference between PRP and GFC?

Both are blood-derived regenerative approaches, but preparation methods differ. The clinician can explain which protocol is suitable for your indication.

Is PRP/GFC enough for hair loss?

Sometimes it is supportive, but many patients also need diagnosis-led medical or scalp treatment.

How many sessions are needed?

The course depends on the concern, severity and response. It should be discussed after assessment.

Is there downtime?

Many patients resume routine activity quickly, but tenderness, redness or mild swelling can occur depending on treatment area.
