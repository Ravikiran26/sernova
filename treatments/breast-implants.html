Before You Book

Questions worth asking.

Clear answers help compare options with confidence.

How is implant size decided?

Chest width, tissue thickness and desired profile are all considered — not just cup size preference.

What is the recovery like?

Discomfort, swelling and activity restriction last several weeks. A support garment is typically worn.

Can implants affect breastfeeding?

This depends on placement and incision choice. It should be discussed if you plan to breastfeed in future.

How long do implants last?

Implants are not lifetime devices. Long-term monitoring and possible replacement should be planned for.
