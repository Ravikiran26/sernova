Prescription weight management under medical supervision.Injectables and oral medication for suitable candidates.

Weight-loss injectables such as GLP-1 receptor agonists and oral prescription medication can support meaningful weight reduction when used under proper medical oversight.

Who May Benefit

Medication-assisted weight loss suits specific profiles.

Injectables and oral medication are appropriate when lifestyle changes alone have not produced sufficient results and surgery is not indicated or desired.

Medical history, current medications and overall health are reviewed before any prescription is made.

Before You Book

Questions worth asking.

Clear answers help compare options with confidence.

Are weight-loss injectables safe?

When prescribed and monitored by a medical professional for suitable candidates, GLP-1 injectables have a well-documented safety profile.

How much weight can I expect to lose?

Results vary by individual, dose and adherence to lifestyle changes. Realistic expectations are discussed at consultation.

What are the side effects?

Nausea, reduced appetite and gastrointestinal effects are common at the start. Dose titration is used to reduce these.

What happens when I stop medication?

Weight regain is possible. A stopping protocol and lifestyle plan help manage this transition.
