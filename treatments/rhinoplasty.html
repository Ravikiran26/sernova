Nose reshaping for facial balance.Natural from every angle.

Rhinoplasty is planned around facial proportions, nasal structure, skin thickness, breathing function and the client’s desired change.

Nose Reshaping

Ways a nose can be reshaped.

The right route depends on what needs to change, whether breathing is involved and how permanent the result should be.

01Surgical

Cosmetic rhinoplasty

Bone and cartilage are reshaped to change bridge height, tip shape, width or overall size in proportion to the face.

Best for lasting change
02Shape + function

Septorhinoplasty

Reshaping is combined with septum or airway correction when breathing is also a concern.

Best for shape and breathing
03Non-surgical

Filler reshaping

Carefully placed filler may camouflage a small hump or lift a drooping tip. It cannot make the nose smaller and needs repeating.

Best for subtle, temporary change
04Revision

Revision rhinoplasty

Corrects concerns after previous surgery and often needs cartilage grafts and more detailed planning.

Best after full healing
What reshaping can change: bridge height and humps, tip projection and rotation, nostril and base width, crookedness after injury, and overall nose size relative to the face.
AI-Assisted Nose Analysis

Your proportions, measured before they are discussed.

During consultation, standardised facial photographs can be analysed with AI-based facial landmark recognition. The software identifies key points on the nose and face and measures how they relate to each other.

These measurements give you and the surgeon a shared, objective starting point. They support the conversation; the surgical plan is always decided by the surgeon after examination.

01

Standardised photos

Front, side and base views taken in consistent lighting.

02

Landmark recognition

AI locates points such as the nasion, tip, alar base and eye corners.

03

Proportion measurement

Nasal width, length, angles and symmetry are compared with facial thirds.

04

Surgeon review

Findings are explained in plain language and checked against examination.

AI supports judgement. It does not replace it.

Measurements and any simulated previews are planning aids, not a guarantee of the final result. Healing, skin thickness and anatomy all influence outcome.

    Before You Book

    Questions worth asking.

    Clear answers help clients compare options with more confidence.

    Can I choose any nose shape?

    The safest and most natural plan depends on your anatomy, skin thickness, facial proportions and breathing function.

    Will breathing be affected?

    Breathing should be discussed before surgery. Functional concerns may change the plan.

    How long does swelling last?

    Visible swelling improves gradually, but final refinement, especially at the tip, can take many months.

    Does the AI decide what my nose should look like?

    No. AI landmark recognition measures proportions to support the discussion. The surgeon examines your nose, skin and breathing and makes every planning decision with you.

    Why doesn't the cost estimator show a price?

    Rhinoplasty fees depend on operating time, technique, cartilage grafts, anaesthesia and stay, so a fixed online price is often misleading. Send your estimator answers on WhatsApp for a personalised quote.

    Is non-surgical nose reshaping permanent?

    No. Filler results are temporary and it cannot reduce nose size or correct breathing. Surgical rhinoplasty is the option for lasting structural change.

    Is revision rhinoplasty possible?

    Sometimes, but revision cases are more complex and need careful examination of structure, scar tissue and expectations.
