Sri Sai Foods Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Sri Sai Foods is a home-based business in Kokapet, Hyderabad, making homemade sweets and snacks. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us or fill in one of our forms, and how we use it.

What we collect

When you fill in our form on Instagram or Facebook, or message us on Instagram or WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your area or delivery address
  • The items you are interested in and any message you send us

Why we collect it

We use your information only to:

  • Contact you about your enquiry or order
  • Confirm, prepare and deliver your order
  • Tell you about our festival specials, only if you have agreed to hear from us

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your information to anyone.
  • We do not share it with anyone, except a delivery partner when needed to deliver your order.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for up to 12 months after your last enquiry or order, and then delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

Your choices and rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • Show you the information we hold about you
  • Correct it
  • Delete it
  • Stop sending you messages

Message us with your request and we will act on it within 7 days.

Contact us

Send us a message on Instagram at @srisaifoods_, or tap the WhatsApp button on our Instagram profile.
